# Seven of those ten hold at least 128 examples after deduplication and so can fill a client at
# the training length the detector reads; adb (114), update-engine (32) and netd (9) cannot, and
# are left out rather than packed into short clients that would not be comparable.
#

# project-level permutation, where `clients-cpp-early.txt` gave 84. The floor a p-value can reach
# falls from 0.012 to 0.0006.
