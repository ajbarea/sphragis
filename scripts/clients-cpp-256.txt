# The third training length: 256 examples a client, on the rebuilt AOSP corpus.
#
# 64 and 128 gave a detector that rises with local training and a classifier that does not
# survive a repacking. Whether the detector keeps rising needs a third point, and 256 is the
# largest this corpus supports: a project must hold 256 usable examples after deduplication
# to fill even one client, which leaves five AOSP C++ projects against four of Qt's,
# C(9, 5) = 126 groupings and a permutation floor of 0.008.
#
# Left out because they cannot fill one client at this size, not by choice: AOSP's bionic
# (191), adb (114), update-engine (32), netd (9) and frameworks-native (239); Qt's qtgraphs
# (281) and qttools (258), too close to the line once refused rows are removed; and every
# OpenStack Python project, the largest of which is starlingx-test at 370 but whose siblings
# are near 200. The Python cell is therefore absent at this length rather than half present,
# since a content control with one organization in it controls nothing.
#
# Corpora live in $CLIENTS = $HOME/corpus/clients3. Paths are relative to $CLIENTS.
